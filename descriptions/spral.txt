SPRAL (Sparse Parallel Robust Algorithms Library) is an open-source
library of sparse linear algebra and associated algorithms from the
Science and Technology Facilities Council (STFC). Its SSIDS solver is a
parallel direct solver for sparse symmetric indefinite systems, used in
the stack by Ipopt (linear_solver spral).
SSIDS requires OMP_CANCELLATION=TRUE (set by the SCLS activate script when
SPRAL is installed); OMP_PROC_BIND=TRUE is recommended for performance.
